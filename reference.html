Mutual Agreement for Document Excellence
A Manifesto, Operating Agreement, and Love Letter to Modern Document Editing
By developers exploring every corner of the OOXML spec
PREAMBLE
Be advised that the following findings are presented in the highest possible tone of unnecessary
legal formality. The Parties enter these statements into the record with full awareness of their
own ambition. The reader is encouraged to nod solemnly throughout.
• WHEREAS the Party of the First Party (hereinafter “The Developer”) dedicated countless
hours to mastering the art of document formatting, with the git history to show for it; and
• WHEREAS traditional Inline contentWYSIWYG editors rely on a pipeline of `DOCX → HTML → hope →
DOCX`, while SuperDoc chose a more elegant path: full and native OOXML from the start;
and
• WHEREAS the Party envisioned a future where document get/load import and document
export always preserve the full document content — a revolutionary concept known in some
circles as “it just works"; and
• WHEREAS The SuperDoc team took on the ambitious mission of mapping 6,000+ pages of
OOXML specification to an elegant editing schema, powered by TypeScript and a deep
appreciation for XML namespaces; and
• WHEREAS the resulting product — SuperDoc — is an open-source, self-hostable,
LLM-ready document editor and tooling that the Parties wish to formally declare as "pretty
darn great."*
* Legal reviewed this language. They wanted 'industry-leading.' Engineering won.

ARTICLE II: Technical foundations
The following technologies constitute the engineering bedrock upon which this Agreement is
built. (They're also the answer to 'what's your tech stack?' at every meetup.)
1. JavaScript / TypeScript — SuperDoc can fully run entirely in the browser or entirely
on the server as a JavaScript library with enhanced TypeScript safety and modern
developer experience.
2. OOXML (ECMA-376) — The specification that keeps on giving. 6,000+ pages of
XML schemas, and we've read most of them. Twice. Voluntarily. We ensure our
documents are fully compatible with real-world MS Word Desktop.
3. LLM Integration
a. True document intelligence.
b. Deep integration that
i. understands document structure,
ii. respects formatting boundaries, and
iii. preserves clean, semantic markup throughout.
c. All this can be directly driven by your own LLM or agentic workflow.
We want to be part of your code. Import SuperDoc as a library or LLM tool, and build
amazing experiences. We handle the real-world MS Word compatibility.
 
ARTICLE III: Recruitment clause
